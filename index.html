About The Company

AL QURWASHI INTERNATIONAL TRADING SPC (QT)

We are a leading Omani Company catering services to the Oil & Gas, EPC, Construction industry, Facility Management, Supply, Rental of Vehicles & Equipment's, Manpower, and Pipeline sectors.

AL QURWASHI operates to high standards of Quality with Health, Safety and Environmental protection. We have located our assets strategically to be able to mobilize manpower, equipment, and provide other services to any part of the Sultanate of Oman.

Our History

AL QURWASHI is a leading 100% Omani owned group of companies with active participation in Oman's Hydrocarbon, Petrochemical, Building Construction, Facility Management, and Manpower Supply sector industries since 2014.

Established initially as Al Qarwashi Manpower in 2014, and later Al Yaqoot Global for Business in 2019. In 2021, Al Qarwashi International Trading was created as a parent company grouping all branches under one sector, currently rated as one of the leading Omani Contractors in the Sultanate of Oman.
